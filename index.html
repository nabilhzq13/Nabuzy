Qadha

Shafi'i school

How qadha works

›
What it is
A fard prayer that isn't prayed before its time ends becomes a debt. You repay it, qadha, by praying it later with the same rakaat: Subuh 2, Zohor 4, Asar 4, Maghrib 3, Isyak 4.
When each time ends
Subuh at syuruk (sunrise). Zohor when Asar comes in. Asar at Maghrib (sunset). Maghrib when Isyak comes in. Isyak at Subuh the next morning, so it stays open past midnight. Al-Kafi #266
How soon
Missed because you slept or forgot: make it up when you remember, and doing it soon is sunat. Missed without an excuse: making it up straight away is wajib. Al-Kafi #1608
Which first
Oldest first: Subuh before Zohor, Zohor before Asar. Keeping that order is sunat, and prayers missed without an excuse go before ones missed with one. If the current prayer's time is safe, it's sunat to make up the missed one first; if it's running out, pray the current one first. Al-Kafi #1608
Any time of day
A missed fard prayer can be made up even after Asar, when sunat prayers are normally avoided. Irsyad al-Fatwa #164
Niat
Name the prayer you're making up, for example:Sahaja aku solat fardu Zohor empat rakaat qadha kerana Allah Taala.
Missed for years
Make them up as far as you're able, a few at a time if need be, until they're done, along with taubat. Count them by your best estimate; if you've lost count, keep going until you're sure none are left. Irsyad Hukum #5 Al-Kafi #2057
Not owed
Prayers missed during haid or nifas are not made up. Irsyad al-Fatwa #287

This follows the Shafi'i school as Malaysia's fatwa offices teach it. For your own situation, ask an ustaz or your state mufti.
